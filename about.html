About Pop Quiz

A simple study tool made for students.

Pop Quiz lets you practise with ready-made class quizzes or build your own. You can create, edit, duplicate, export and delete quizzes, and keep a history of your scores.

The site is fully static: it uses plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and saves everything in your browser’s local storage. There is no server and no database.

Questions and answers

Where are my quizzes saved?

In this browser on this device. If you clear your browser data they will be removed, so use Export to make a backup file.

How do I share a quiz with a friend?

Open My quizzes, click Export, and send the .json file. Your friend uses Import JSON on the same page.

Can I edit the built-in quizzes?

No, but you can duplicate one from My quizzes and edit the copy.

How is the grade worked out?

A+ is 100%, A is 80% or more, B is 60% or more, C is 40% or more, and D is below that.
